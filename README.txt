PAINTING & REMODELING LEAD WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
2. Replace FreshCo Home, phone, email, service area, project photos, reviews, and policies with your real business information.
3. The form is currently a demo. Connect it to your CRM, email/webhook, or backend endpoint to receive real leads.
4. For paid traffic/SEO, create dedicated pages for each service and each service area.
5. Do not scrape or purchase personal contact information without a lawful basis; use the site to capture inbound homeowner inquiries and qualify them.

The visual/content structure was adapted from the uploaded Style2Paints README's workflow/examples approach, but the business content is specifically for painting/remodeling lead generation.
